How to Play

Click Start Game below to begin your game.

You have three chances total to get one of the following two sets of numbers:
➤ 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
➤ 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

Click Keep? to hold on to a dice (for your set).
Click Roll Dice to roll more dice in available slots.
Click Reset Game to reset the game and try again.
(These three buttons will be clickable when you play)

You have roll(s) available.
